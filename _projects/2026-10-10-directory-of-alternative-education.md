**The Directory of Alternative Education** is an emergent mailing list of artist-run and broadly para/extra-institutional schools.

This list grew out of an recurring event series at the [Antiuniversity Festival]() about artist-run schools ([available to watch on Youtube](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLfw1pM6mwqc7kEMq4T_KpFh4hcpGsKFDG&si=LEIOCVPfszTVQht6)).

By creating a shared space for these educational environments, this mailing list hopes to provide an opportunity to share tactics, ways of working, resources, and experiences.
